AI governance

How Ailane uses AI

Ailane's analysis and its assistant, Eileen, use AI models provided by Anthropic (Claude) through Anthropic's commercial API. Every Ailane output that is produced with AI is labelled as such.

Personal data and the AI provider

Before a document is analysed, we remove names and other identifiers we can detect. If we cannot do this confidently, the document is not analysed and we ask for a template or a redacted copy instead. Anthropic states that it deletes inputs and outputs sent through its commercial API within 30 days, subject to limited exceptions described in its own policies, and that it does not train its models on commercial customer content. Uploaded documents are deleted from Ailane's systems once your report is produced.

Human review and transparency

Ailane provides regulatory intelligence, not legal advice, and does not make decisions about individuals. Where our output informs advice to, or a decision about, a specific person, our terms require it to be reviewed first by someone in your organisation with the authority and experience to depart from it, and the individual should be told that AI-assisted analysis was used. Eileen prompts both steps and can draft the note for you.

Questions

For questions about how we use AI or personal data, contact enquiries@ailane.ai. See also our Privacy Policy.
