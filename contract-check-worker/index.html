Help for employees and workers

Ailane provides regulatory intelligence to employers and to the advisers who support them. We do not offer contract checks to employees or workers reviewing their own terms of employment.

If you have a question about your own employment, these organisations offer impartial information and support:

  • Acas — information and advice on rights at work, and early conciliation (England, Scotland and Wales).
  • Citizens Advice — help with problems at work.
  • Labour Relations Agency — information and advice for people working in Northern Ireland.
