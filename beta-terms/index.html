AI Lane Limited, registered in England and Wales, Companies House 17035654, registered office 4 Min-Y-Mor, Barry, CF62 6QG ("AI Lane"). Published at ailane.ai/beta-terms/.

This Addendum applies to business customers only. It sets the terms of AI Lane's Design Partner Programme (the "Programme"). It supplements the Master Services Agreement at ailane.ai/msa/ (the "MSA"), the Data Processing Agreement at ailane.ai/dpa/, the Acceptable Use Policy at ailane.ai/aup/ and the Terms of Service at ailane.ai/terms/. Capitalised terms have the meanings given in the MSA.

1. Application and order of precedence

1.1 This Addendum applies to an organisation that AI Lane has invited to the Programme and that has accepted the invitation (a "Design Partner"), and to its Authorised Users, for the period of complimentary access under clause 3.

1.2 This Addendum is accepted by click-acceptance in the same way as the MSA, with each version identified by a version number and SHA-256 content hash.

1.3 If this Addendum conflicts with the MSA, the DPA, the Acceptable Use Policy or the Terms of Service, this Addendum prevails for the Programme only. In every other respect those documents apply unchanged. Nothing in this Addendum varies the DPA's protection of personal data.

1.4 The Programme is not offered to consumers. By accepting this Addendum the person accepting confirms that they act for the Design Partner in the course of its business and have authority to bind it.

2. Pre-release services

2.1 The Programme gives access to the Services, which may include features that are still in development ("Pre-release Features"). Pre-release Features are identified as such in the platform.

2.2 AI Lane may change, suspend or withdraw any Pre-release Feature at any time. During the Programme AI Lane gives no commitment as to the availability or performance of the Services.

2.3 The Services provide regulatory intelligence. They are not legal advice and are not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer on the Design Partner's own circumstances (Terms of Service clause 3.1).

2.4 The Design Partner should verify any output before relying on it for a decision with legal or financial consequences, and should not use Pre-release Features as its only basis for such a decision.

2.5 To the extent permitted by law, Pre-release Features are provided "as is", and the warranties in MSA clause 12 do not apply to them.

3. Complimentary access

3.1 Access is complimentary for 90 days from the date AI Lane activates the Design Partner's account (the "Access Period"), at the tier stated in the invitation.

3.2 Between day 60 and day 75 AI Lane will review the Programme with the Design Partner. AI Lane may, at its discretion, extend the Access Period once, to a total of 180 days, by notice to the Design Partner.

3.3 No payment details are required. No fee is payable for the Access Period. Access does not convert automatically to a paid subscription; any paid subscription requires a separate order accepted by the Design Partner.

3.4 The Access Period is not transferable and has no cash value.

4. Participation and feedback

4.1 AI Lane asks the Design Partner to take part in an onboarding call, to give feedback around day 30 and at the review under clause 3.2, and to give a closing view, including on pricing. Participation is voluntary, and the Design Partner may decline any request.

4.2 The Design Partner grants AI Lane a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use, adapt and incorporate any suggestion, comment or other feedback it gives about the Services ("Feedback") to develop and improve AI Lane's products. AI Lane will not identify the Design Partner as the source of Feedback without its prior written consent.

4.3 Feedback must not include personal data or the Design Partner's confidential information unless it is needed and AI Lane has agreed to receive it. Personal data that is provided is handled under the Privacy Policy and, where AI Lane processes it for the Design Partner, the DPA.

5. References and publicity

5.1 AI Lane will not name the Design Partner, use its name or logo, or quote it as a reference or in any publicity without the Design Partner's prior written consent, which may be withheld or withdrawn at any time for future use.

5.2 The Design Partner may say publicly that it is taking part in the Programme. It may not publish benchmarks or comparative performance results about Pre-release Features without AI Lane's prior written consent.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Pre-release Features, Programme pricing discussions and any non-public information AI Lane shares about its roadmap are AI Lane's confidential information under the MSA.

6.2 The MSA's confidentiality obligations apply in both directions, including its exceptions for information that is public, already known, independently developed or required to be disclosed by law.

7. Liability

7.1 Nothing in this Addendum limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

7.2 Subject to clause 7.1, neither party is liable to the other under or in connection with the Programme for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue, business, goodwill or anticipated savings.

7.3 Subject to clauses 7.1 and 7.4, each party's total aggregate liability to the other under or in connection with the Programme, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, is limited to £1,000.

7.4 The limit in clause 7.3 does not apply to: (a) a party's liability for breach of clause 6 (confidentiality); (b) a party's liability under the DPA or data protection law, which the DPA governs; or (c) the Design Partner's liability for breach of the Acceptable Use Policy or infringement of AI Lane's intellectual property.

7.5 The parties agree these limits are reasonable, taking into account that the Services are provided free of charge, include Pre-release Features and are offered so that the Design Partner can evaluate them.

8. Data on the platform

8.1 The Programme does not change how personal data is processed. The Privacy Policy, the Tribunal Data Privacy Notice and the DPA apply in full.

8.2 When the Access Period ends without a paid subscription, the Design Partner may export its data for 30 days. After that period AI Lane deletes it in line with the Privacy Policy and the DPA, unless the law requires AI Lane to keep it.

9. Ending the Programme

9.1 Either party may end the Design Partner's participation at any time by notice. AI Lane will give at least 7 days' notice unless clause 9.2 applies.

9.2 AI Lane may suspend or end access immediately for a breach of the Acceptable Use Policy, or where reasonably necessary to address a serious security, legal or integrity risk (Acceptable Use Policy clause 10).

9.3 Participation ends automatically at the end of the Access Period unless the Design Partner has taken out a paid subscription. Clauses 4.2, 5, 6, 7, 8.2 and 10 continue after it ends.

10. General

10.1 AI Lane may change this Addendum by publishing a new version under the change mechanism in MSA clause 5. A change does not reduce an Access Period already granted.

10.2 This Addendum and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the law of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

10.3 Questions about the Programme: partnerships@ailane.ai. Complaints follow the Complaint Handling Policy at ailane.ai/complaints/.
